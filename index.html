Calibre V-01, 312 components

The movement, laid bare.

Every bridge is cut from a single plate, then bevelled and satin-finished by hand. Nothing is hidden because nothing is decorative. Touch the markers to read the watch.

“TIME IS NOT MEASURED. IT IS ENGINEERED.”

A second is a promise made by a balance wheel, 28,800 times an hour. We build the machinery that keeps it, in small series, with tolerances measured in microns and finishing that takes longer than the assembly.

We draw our references from aerospace and motorsport: grade 5 titanium, forged carbon, sapphire cut to a flat plane. The result is a watch that is lighter than it looks and calmer than it should be.

The Atelier

Eleven watchmakers share one bench room. A single movement passes through four pairs of hands, and leaves only when the person who started it signs the case back.
